About Toon Minerals

Toon Minerals, LLC is a family owned mineral purchasing and management company based in Austin, Texas. We work with our owners and other mineral buying partnerships to invest in all types of oil and gas assets across the United States.

Because we invest our own capital, we can move quickly and make offers on interests of any size — from a single fractional royalty interest to a large multi-basin package. We can also undertake title work on a case-by-case basis for a fixed negotiated fee.

What We Do

Toon Minerals buys, manages, and researches oil and gas mineral and royalty interests nationwide.

Mineral & Royalty Acquisition

We purchase mineral rights, royalty interests, overriding royalties, and non-operated working interests across every major U.S. producing basin. No interest is too small.

Mineral Management

We manage mineral and royalty portfolios for our owners and partners, including revenue tracking, lease administration, division order review, and ownership recordkeeping.

Title Work

We undertake oil and gas title research and chain-of-title work on a case-by-case basis for a fixed negotiated fee. Contact us to discuss scope and pricing.
